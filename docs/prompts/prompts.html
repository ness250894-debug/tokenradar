TokenRadar AI Prompting Strategy

This generated artifact pair is the source of record for TokenRadar's article-generation prompt contract. The retired root PROMPTS.md source has been removed; keep docs/prompts/prompts.json and docs/prompts/prompts.html updated together.

This document covers the website article prompt flow. The broader queue, publishing, editorial, schema, and integration flows are documented in docs/pipeline/, docs/editorial/, docs/data-schema/, and docs/integrations/.

1. Prompt Flow Boundaries

  • scripts/generate-content.ts --drip --queue builds article prompts and writes generated drafts into data/queue/.
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash is the primary article generation model through callAIWithFallback(); Claude Haiku 4.5 is the fallback when Gemini cannot complete the request.
  • Standard token articles use SYSTEM_PROMPT; launch articles use the separate TGE_SYSTEM_PROMPT.
  • Each generated article is parsed from the explicit ---TITLE---, ---CONTENT---, and ---END--- output envelope.
  • Generation retries up to 3 times when parsing or blocking quality checks fail.
  • The prompt flow does not publish directly. Publication, placeholder hydration, link injection, repair, validation, build, and deploy happen downstream.

2. Shared Editorial Rules

These rules apply to all generated website articles unless a TGE-specific rule is stricter:

  1. Tone must be professional, analytical, data-driven, and free of hype or FOMO.
  2. Never recommend buying or selling a token.
  3. Never guarantee returns, profits, safety, or exact future prices.
  4. Use objective scenario language such as "historical patterns suggest", "based on current data", or "in a bullish scenario".
  5. Include at least 3 specific numerical data points from provided context, excluding unresolved live placeholders.
  6. Reference real source context when available, but do not copy source snippets.
  7. Use only ## Markdown headings in generated article content.
  8. Include a useful introductory paragraph before the first heading.
  9. Include a Markdown summary table early in the article.
  10. Include a final ## FAQ section with 3 to 5 questions.
  11. End with the exact standard disclaimer:
---
*Disclaimer: This article is for informational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Always do your own research (DYOR).*
  1. Do not include external URLs, ads, or third-party domains in generated copy. Internal links are injected after generation.
  2. Do not bold full paragraphs. Bold only short phrases when emphasis is useful.

3. Standard Article Placeholders

Standard token articles must use exact placeholders for volatile live market fields during generation. scripts/publish-from-queue.ts hydrates these immediately before writing approved content to content/tokens/.

Placeholder Publish-time value
{{LIVE_PRICE}} Current price with a dollar prefix
{{LIVE_MARKET_CAP}} Current market cap with a dollar prefix
{{LIVE_RANK}} Current market cap rank
{{LIVE_DATE}} Current date formatted as Month Day, Year
{{LIVE_24H_CHANGE}} 24-hour percentage change
{{GLOBAL_MCAP}} Global crypto market cap
{{GLOBAL_TOTAL_MARKET_CAP}} Global crypto market cap alias
{{BTC_DOM}} BTC dominance
{{GLOBAL_BTC_DOMINANCE}} BTC dominance alias

The quality layer warns if live placeholders remain in generated article content. The publisher blocks and deletes queued files that still contain unresolved placeholders after hydration.

4. TGE Prompt Exception

TGE preview articles use TGE_SYSTEM_PROMPT, not the standard market-data prompt.

  • Do not use {{LIVE_PRICE}}, {{LIVE_MARKET_CAP}}, {{LIVE_RANK}}, {{LIVE_DATE}}, or {{LIVE_24H_CHANGE}}.
  • If the token is not actively trading, say market data is not available yet instead of inventing price, market cap, exchange listings, or liquidity.
  • Treat narrative strength as attention, not proof.
  • Only call a launch confirmed when the TGE lifecycle status supports that claim.
  • Use TGE signals, confidence, source count, contracts, chains, tokenomics, and GeckoTerminal data only when provided.
  • Do not invent investors, tokenomics, contracts, chains, exchange listings, official links, or launch dates.
  • Include a compact evidence table early in the article.

Pre-launch TGE publication may use explicit TBA market fields when no live pool exists. Normal token publication is blocked when live market data cannot be fetched.

5. Injected Context Blocks

Article prompts can receive the following context blocks:

  • TOKEN DATA: CoinGecko token detail data such as market cap, supply, rank, categories, descriptions, and market fields.
  • PROPRIETARY METRICS: TokenRadar metrics such as risk score, growth potential, narrative strength, volatility, and related computed fields.
  • PRICE HISTORY SUMMARY: 30-day and 1-year high, low, average, and percentage-change data.
  • REFERENCE ARTICLES: Stored source snippets for factual grounding and style reference only.
  • SEMANTIC CLUSTERING RULE: Related token names that must be compared against the target token when available.
  • GLOBAL MARKET CONTEXT: Total market cap, BTC dominance, and sector performance used for strategic grounding.
  • TGE ENTRY DATA: Discovery-pipeline fields for pre-launch or launch-transition projects.
  • DEX LIVE MARKET DATA: GeckoTerminal price, volume, liquidity, FDV, DEX, pool age, and 24-hour change when a TGE pool is found.

If reference articles are missing, the prompt explicitly says no recent articles were found. If related-token lookup fails, generation continues without the semantic comparison rule.

6. Article Archetypes

Type Prompt target Prompt focus Special constraints
overview 1,000-1,200 words Fundamentals, architecture, tokenomics, TokenRadar metrics, risks, recent developments Must explain the token without turning source descriptions into marketing copy.
price-prediction 1,000-1,200 words Historical price movement, ATH/ATL context, support and resistance, bear/base/bull scenarios Must not make exact price predictions or single-target forecasts.
how-to-buy 800-1,000 words Market availability checks, pair/network/contract verification, fees, slippage, custody, taxes Must not claim Binance, Coinbase, Bybit, Kraken, OKX, Gate.io, KuCoin, MEXC, Bitget, or any other venue lists the asset unless token data verifies it.
tge-preview 800-1,000 words Launch status, confidence, evidence timeline, category, narrative strength, expected launch window Must use the TGE evidence contract and must not use standard live market placeholders.

The quality gate uses lower fail thresholds than the prompt targets: overview and price-analysis articles fail below 800 words, how-to-buy guides fail below 700 words, and TGE previews fail below 500 words.

7. Quality Gates and Retry Loop

During generation, each parsed draft is evaluated by evaluateArticleQuality(). Blocking issues include:

  • Word count below the article-type minimum.
  • Missing early summary table.
  • Missing ## FAQ section or an FAQ outside the 3 to 5 question range.
  • Missing disclaimer.
  • Fewer than 3 numerical data points.
  • Prohibited financial advice, certainty, hype, or promotional phrases.
  • Repeated filler paragraphs.
  • Malformed Markdown table blocks.

If a draft fails parsing or blocking quality checks, generation retries with the same article type. Successful articles store a fresh quality snapshot in the generated article JSON.

Warnings include borderline word count, long average sentence length, unsupported heading depth, weak opening summaries, missing internal continuation links, hardcoded live-date phrasing, exact-disclaimer drift, unresolved live placeholders, and excessive generic filler. Warnings are not automatic generation blockers.

8. Publication and Post-Generation Mutation

Prompt output is only the first draft. The downstream flow can still modify or drop content:

  1. scripts/quality-check.ts --dir data/queue --fix --delete-failures applies queue gates and limited fixes.
  2. scripts/publish-from-queue.ts --max hydrates placeholders, blocks unresolved placeholders, rebuilds quality snapshots, and writes approved articles to content/tokens/.
  3. scripts/inject-internal-links.ts adds approved internal links after generation.
  4. scripts/repair-article-content.ts normalizes Markdown, neutralizes unsupported how-to-buy venue claims, unwraps ambiguous internal links, moves or adds the disclaimer, inserts continuation sections, deepens thin articles, and refreshes quality metadata.
  5. scripts/validate-content.ts validates JSON syntax, article path/type/slug integrity, quality gates, outbound URL policy, TGE references, and schema contracts before build and deploy.

Because post-generation repair can change content, the prompt contract should be read together with docs/editorial/ and docs/pipeline/.

9. Social Prompt Boundary

Social posts, polls, captions, video scripts, and platform variants are not governed by this website article prompt contract. They use social-specific instructions in src/lib/gemini.ts, src/lib/social-variety.ts, src/lib/social-content-generator.ts, and the scripts/post-*.ts automation files.

The social layer still inherits the same no-advice, no-hype, no-unsupported-venue-claim editorial boundary.
