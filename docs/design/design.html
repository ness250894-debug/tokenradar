Design System: NEURAL_CMD (Technical Intelligence)

Project ID: TokenRadar-Web Version: 2.0 (Neural Update)

1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

NEURAL_CMD employs a "Technical Brutalism meets Contextual Intelligence" aesthetic. The atmosphere is industrial, high-density, and performance-oriented, relying on deep obsidian layers contrasted with high-visibility neon accents. The UI utilizes sub-second latency visualizations, hairline borders, and progressive disclosure to handle complex quantitative research. The design prioritizes "Data as Art," where grid lines and technical metrics form the core visual structure.

2. Color Palette & Roles

  • Obsidian Surface (#07080B): Root background and deep structural layers.
  • Coal Panel (#141414): Elevated card surfaces, sidebars, and contextual panels.
  • Neon Chartreuse (#CCFF00): Primary brand accent. Active states, critical data points, primary actions, and branding highlights.
  • Cyber Blue (#00E5FF): Secondary accent. Informational tooltips, secondary links, and neutral technical data.
  • Emerald Growth (#00FFA3): Semantic color for bullish sentiment, growth index, and security verification.
  • Warning Red (#FF3366): Semantic color for bearish signals, high risk, and anomalies.
  • Slate Muted (#4A4A4A): Secondary labels, inactive grid lines, and timestamps.
  • Technical Border (rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.15)): Subtle hairline dividers and component boundaries.

3. Typography Rules

  • Primary Font (Sans-Serif): Outfit. Used for high-level headings and brand identity to maintain a sleek, modern geometric feel.
  • Secondary Font (Monospace): JetBrains Mono. Used for the majority of data, pricing, technical metrics, and "terminal" style outputs to ensure perfect vertical alignment.
  • Headers: Extra Bold (font-weight: 800), featuring tight letter spacing (letter-spacing: -0.03em) for a compact, authoritative technical look.
  • Data Labels: Monospaced, bold (font-weight: 700), uppercase, with precise tracking (letter-spacing: 0.05em).
  • Body Copy: Highly legible (Base: 16px), with a controlled line-height (1.5) for efficient consumption of research data.

4. Component Stylings

  • Action Nodes (Buttons):
  • Primary: Sharp or slightly rounded corners (4px). Uses a solid Neon Chartreuse background with deep Obsidian text. No gradients. Hover state intensifies the glow effect.
  • Ghost Node: Transparent background with a Neon Chartreuse hairline border. Hover fills the node with a 10% opacity Chartreuse tint.
  • Data Modules (Cards):
  • Standard: Minimal corner radius (8px). Built on Coal Surface with a Chartreuse Technical Border. Hover state triggers a subtle scale-up and border intensity increase.
  • Highlight: Features a left-accent border of Neon Chartreuse or Cyber Blue to denote priority.
  • Inputs & Controls:
  • Technical Sliders: Coal tracks with Neon Chartreuse thumbs. Thumbs are square or diamond-shaped to emphasize the brutalist aesthetic.
  • Grid Tabs: Flat, rectangular segments with a high-contrast Neon Chartreuse indicator for the active state.
  • Status Indicators: Small geometric shapes (circles/squares) using Emerald Growth or Warning Red to provide instant sentiment feedback.

5. Layout Principles

  • Data Density: Optimized for high information density. Elements are packed efficiently with a focus on logical grouping and visual hierarchy.
  • The Grid: All layouts are strictly aligned to an 8px grid system. Grid lines are often visible or implied through consistent spacing.
  • Responsive Scaling: Fluid transitions between Mobile (1 column), Tablet (2 columns), and Desktop (1200px max-width centered or fluid depending on the module).
  • Information Architecture: Progressive disclosure is used to hide deep complexity until requested, preventing user overwhelm while maintaining technical depth.

6. Automated Visual Assets

All dynamically generated assets (OG images, Market Reports, Videos) must adhere to the core palette:

  • Backgrounds: Always use Obsidian (#07080B) with subtle Coal (#141414) radial gradients or 40px/40px Neon Chartreuse grid overlays at 3-5% opacity.
  • Typography: Maintain the hierarchy of Outfit for headings and JetBrains Mono for data points.
  • Semantic Consistency: Risk scores and price changes must strictly use Emerald (#00FFA3), Warning Red (#FF3366), or Vibrant Yellow (#FFB800) based on value thresholds defined in src/lib/formatters.ts.
  • Branding: The TokenRadar logo or URL (TokenRadar.co) should be present in Neon Chartreuse, typically with a high-contrast weight.

7. Product Flow Coverage

The visual rules above must be paired with flow coverage for the live TokenRadar surfaces. The design system is responsible for defining user movement, states, and trust cues across src/app routes, not only component appearance.

  • Route Map: Cover the home dashboard, token directory, token detail pages, category pages, Search Intent dashboard and detail pages, upcoming launch tracker and detail pages, watchlist, learn/editorial pages, wallet and tax partner pages, contact, offline fallback, and social authorization callbacks.
  • Primary Journeys: Document the expected path from homepage discovery to directory filtering, token research, watchlist save, alert subscription, related research, and external social or partner handoff. Upcoming launch journeys must include discovery, evidence review, alert follow, graduation to a live token page, and stale or candidate review.
  • Search, Filter, and Sort States: Specify interaction rules for token and TGE search, category/risk/intent/signal filters, sort controls, load-more pagination, no-results states, and filter reset behavior.
  • Watchlist States: Cover local-storage loading, saved, empty, imported, exported, cleared, invalid import, missing saved IDs, cross-device export messaging, and offline behavior.
  • Data Trust States: Provide visual treatments for fresh, stale, partial, unavailable, unverified, candidate, confirmed, graduated, needs-review, high-risk, and anomalous market data. Timestamps, source evidence, risk scores, article quality gates, and method notes should use consistent hierarchy.
  • Error and Recovery States: Define contact form sending, success, mailto fallback, network failure, data-fetch gaps, empty content, missing articles, missing charts, authorization callback success/error/no-code states, and offline recovery links.
  • Responsive and Accessibility Rules: Extend the mobile/tablet/desktop guidance with keyboard focus, visible focus rings, reduced-motion behavior, menu open/close states, chart readability, form validation, touch targets, and screen-reader labels for icon-only controls.
  • CTA and Analytics Hand-Offs: Define how Telegram alerts, X/TikTok/Threads/Instagram links, affiliate cards, research recirculation modules, and contact submissions appear, confirm intent, and preserve disclosure language.

# Design System: NEURAL_CMD (Technical Intelligence)
**Project ID:** TokenRadar-Web
**Version:** 2.0 (Neural Update)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
NEURAL_CMD employs a "Technical Brutalism meets Contextual Intelligence" aesthetic. The atmosphere is industrial, high-density, and performance-oriented, relying on deep obsidian layers contrasted with high-visibility neon accents. The UI utilizes sub-second latency visualizations, hairline borders, and progressive disclosure to handle complex quantitative research. The design prioritizes "Data as Art," where grid lines and technical metrics form the core visual structure.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
*   **Obsidian Surface** (#07080B): Root background and deep structural layers.
*   **Coal Panel** (#141414): Elevated card surfaces, sidebars, and contextual panels.
*   **Neon Chartreuse** (#CCFF00): Primary brand accent. Active states, critical data points, primary actions, and branding highlights.
*   **Cyber Blue** (#00E5FF): Secondary accent. Informational tooltips, secondary links, and neutral technical data.
*   **Emerald Growth** (#00FFA3): Semantic color for bullish sentiment, growth index, and security verification.
*   **Warning Red** (#FF3366): Semantic color for bearish signals, high risk, and anomalies.
*   **Slate Muted** (#4A4A4A): Secondary labels, inactive grid lines, and timestamps.
*   **Technical Border** (rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.15)): Subtle hairline dividers and component boundaries.

## 3. Typography Rules
*   **Primary Font (Sans-Serif):** *Outfit*. Used for high-level headings and brand identity to maintain a sleek, modern geometric feel.
*   **Secondary Font (Monospace):** *JetBrains Mono*. Used for the majority of data, pricing, technical metrics, and "terminal" style outputs to ensure perfect vertical alignment.
*   **Headers:** Extra Bold (`font-weight: 800`), featuring tight letter spacing (`letter-spacing: -0.03em`) for a compact, authoritative technical look.
*   **Data Labels:** Monospaced, bold (`font-weight: 700`), uppercase, with precise tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`).
*   **Body Copy:** Highly legible (Base: 16px), with a controlled line-height (1.5) for efficient consumption of research data.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Action Nodes (Buttons):**
    *   **Primary:** Sharp or slightly rounded corners (`4px`). Uses a solid Neon Chartreuse background with deep Obsidian text. No gradients. Hover state intensifies the glow effect.
    *   **Ghost Node:** Transparent background with a Neon Chartreuse hairline border. Hover fills the node with a 10% opacity Chartreuse tint.
*   **Data Modules (Cards):**
    *   **Standard:** Minimal corner radius (`8px`). Built on Coal Surface with a Chartreuse Technical Border. Hover state triggers a subtle scale-up and border intensity increase.
    *   **Highlight:** Features a left-accent border of Neon Chartreuse or Cyber Blue to denote priority.
*   **Inputs & Controls:**
    *   **Technical Sliders:** Coal tracks with Neon Chartreuse thumbs. Thumbs are square or diamond-shaped to emphasize the brutalist aesthetic.
    *   **Grid Tabs:** Flat, rectangular segments with a high-contrast Neon Chartreuse indicator for the active state.
*   **Status Indicators:** Small geometric shapes (circles/squares) using Emerald Growth or Warning Red to provide instant sentiment feedback.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Data Density:** Optimized for high information density. Elements are packed efficiently with a focus on logical grouping and visual hierarchy.
*   **The Grid:** All layouts are strictly aligned to an 8px grid system. Grid lines are often visible or implied through consistent spacing.
*   **Responsive Scaling:** Fluid transitions between Mobile (1 column), Tablet (2 columns), and Desktop (1200px max-width centered or fluid depending on the module).
*   **Information Architecture:** Progressive disclosure is used to hide deep complexity until requested, preventing user overwhelm while maintaining technical depth.

## 6. Automated Visual Assets
All dynamically generated assets (OG images, Market Reports, Videos) must adhere to the core palette:
*   **Backgrounds:** Always use Obsidian (#07080B) with subtle Coal (#141414) radial gradients or 40px/40px Neon Chartreuse grid overlays at 3-5% opacity.
*   **Typography:** Maintain the hierarchy of Outfit for headings and JetBrains Mono for data points. 
*   **Semantic Consistency:** Risk scores and price changes must strictly use Emerald (#00FFA3), Warning Red (#FF3366), or Vibrant Yellow (#FFB800) based on value thresholds defined in `src/lib/formatters.ts`.
*   **Branding:** The TokenRadar logo or URL (TokenRadar.co) should be present in Neon Chartreuse, typically with a high-contrast weight.

## 7. Product Flow Coverage
The visual rules above must be paired with flow coverage for the live TokenRadar surfaces. The design system is responsible for defining user movement, states, and trust cues across `src/app` routes, not only component appearance.

*   **Route Map:** Cover the home dashboard, token directory, token detail pages, category pages, Search Intent dashboard and detail pages, upcoming launch tracker and detail pages, watchlist, learn/editorial pages, wallet and tax partner pages, contact, offline fallback, and social authorization callbacks.
*   **Primary Journeys:** Document the expected path from homepage discovery to directory filtering, token research, watchlist save, alert subscription, related research, and external social or partner handoff. Upcoming launch journeys must include discovery, evidence review, alert follow, graduation to a live token page, and stale or candidate review.
*   **Search, Filter, and Sort States:** Specify interaction rules for token and TGE search, category/risk/intent/signal filters, sort controls, load-more pagination, no-results states, and filter reset behavior.
*   **Watchlist States:** Cover local-storage loading, saved, empty, imported, exported, cleared, invalid import, missing saved IDs, cross-device export messaging, and offline behavior.
*   **Data Trust States:** Provide visual treatments for fresh, stale, partial, unavailable, unverified, candidate, confirmed, graduated, needs-review, high-risk, and anomalous market data. Timestamps, source evidence, risk scores, article quality gates, and method notes should use consistent hierarchy.
*   **Error and Recovery States:** Define contact form sending, success, mailto fallback, network failure, data-fetch gaps, empty content, missing articles, missing charts, authorization callback success/error/no-code states, and offline recovery links.
*   **Responsive and Accessibility Rules:** Extend the mobile/tablet/desktop guidance with keyboard focus, visible focus rings, reduced-motion behavior, menu open/close states, chart readability, form validation, touch targets, and screen-reader labels for icon-only controls.
*   **CTA and Analytics Hand-Offs:** Define how Telegram alerts, X/TikTok/Threads/Instagram links, affiliate cards, research recirculation modules, and contact submissions appear, confirm intent, and preserve disclosure language.
